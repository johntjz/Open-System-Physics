### 1. The Bureaucratic Need for "Standardization" and Institutional Legitimacy

When governments and academic boards decided to integrate TCM into modern universities (starting heavily in mainland China in the 1950s and later spreading globally), they faced a massive political hurdle: how to make an empirical, observational, thermodynamics-based craft look "scientific" to Western-educated bureaucrats and medical boards.

- **The Rigid Compartmentalization:** To gain academic respectability, university programs had to adopt the rigid, compartmentalized structure of modern medical schools.
- **The Sterilization of Fluid Physics:** They turned a fluid, highly contextual system of physics and energetic vectors into a sterile set of rigid syllabi: memorize symptom A, match it to pattern B, prescribe textbook formula C.
- **Buried Reality:** The messy, living reality of human thermodynamics—which requires observing a person's color, temperature, environment, and response to weather—got buried under mountains of dry, decontextualized theory designed to pass standard multiple-choice exams.

### 2. The Westernization of Diagnosis (Pattern Differentiation Without Physics)

In university clinics, students are taught "Differential Diagnosis" (*Bian Zheng Lun Zhi* 辨证论治) as an intellectual exercise on paper.

- **Checklist Medicine:** They learn to check boxes: “Pale tongue, thin white coat, floating pulse = Wind-Cold.”
- **Environmental Blindness:** But because the entire academic environment is itself climate-controlled, sanitized, and disconnected from the physical elements, the students never actually connect those abstract textbook terms to real-world physics.
- **The Thermodynamic Failure:** They don't feel the chill of the air-conditioning or look at a patient sitting in a summer T-shirt in October as a thermodynamic failure. They just view it as a textbook case study, completely missing that the disease is actively being generated by the patient's immediate environment and lifestyle habits.

### 3. The Eradication of Lineage and Empirical Real-World Testing

Traditional Chinese medicine was historically preserved through rigorous, clinical lineage transmission—master-apprentice relationships where you watched herbs fail or succeed in real time against the brutal elements of rural life, poverty, and seasonal shifts.

- **Career Academics vs. Field Clinicians:** University professors, by contrast, are often career academics who spend their lives inside air-conditioned lecture halls writing papers on molecular pathways of herbal extracts to satisfy grant requirements.
- **Trading Effectiveness for Safety:** They have traded the gritty, clinical effectiveness of *Shanghan Lun* pragmatism for academic safety. If a professor starts telling students that modern air-conditioning, ice-cold Cokes, and systemic lifestyle compliance are the primary drivers of modern chronic illness, they aren't playing the institutional game. It's much safer to teach pharmacology, chemical constituents, and sanitized acupuncture points that won't ruffle any modern feathers.

When you institutionalize an empirical craft, you strip away its teeth. The university system teaches students how to talk about TCM using acceptable, modern academic language, but it systematically filters out the raw, unvarnished physical truth that the ancients understood: life is fire, cold is death, and ignoring the basic laws of thermodynamics to chase corporate convenience is an absolute straight line to ruin.

### 1. 「標準化」與體制合法性的官僚需求

當政府與學術委員會決定將中醫納入現代大學體系（主要自 1950 年代中國大陸開始，隨後全球化普及）時，他們面臨了一個巨大的政治障礙：如何讓這門以經驗、觀察與熱力學為基礎的技藝，在受過西方教育的官僚與醫療委員會眼中看起來「科學」。

- **僵化的分科體制：** 為了獲得學術尊嚴，大學課程不得不採用現代醫學院那種僵化、細分結構。
- **流體物理學的無菌化：** 他們將一個流動且高度依賴情境的物理與能量向量系統，變成了無菌且死板的課綱：背誦症狀 A、對應證型 B、開立教科書方劑 C。
- **被埋沒的現實：** 人類熱力學紛繁雜亂的真實面貌——需要觀察人的膚色、溫度、環境與對天氣的反應——被埋在旨在通過標準化多項選擇題的大量乾燥且去脈絡化的理論之下。

### 2. 診斷的西方化（沒有物理學的辨證論治）

在大學診所裡，學生被教導將「辨證論治」當作紙上的智力練習。

- **清單式醫學：** 他們學會勾選方格：「舌淡苔白薄、脈浮＝風寒」。
- **環境盲目症：** 但由於整個學術環境本身就是恆溫、無菌且與自然元素脫節的，學生從未真正將這些抽象的教科書術語與真實世界的物理學連結起來。
- **熱力學的失靈：** 他們不會將冷氣的寒意或十月穿著夏季 T 恤坐在診間的病人視為熱力學的失敗。他們只將其視為教科書上的案例研究，完全忽略了疾病其實是由病人的即時環境與生活習慣主動生成的。

### 3. 師承與經驗實證真實考驗的根除

傳統中醫歷史上是透過嚴格的臨床師承傳承下來的——在這種師徒關係中，你能在農村生活、貧困與季節交替的殘酷現實中，即時見證草藥的失敗或成功。

- **職業學者與臨床醫師的對比：** 相反地，大學教授通常是職業學者，他們一生待在有空調的講堂裡，為了滿足研究補助的資格而撰寫關於草藥萃取物分子途徑的論文。
- **以安全換取臨床效能：** 他們用《傷寒論》實用主義那種堅韌的臨床效能，換取了學術上的安全。如果教授開始告訴學生，現代空調、冰鎮可樂與系統性的生活型態順從是現代慢性病的主要驅動力，他們就是沒有在玩體制的遊戲。教導藥理學、化學成分以及不會惹惱任何現代人的無菌針灸穴位，要安全得多。

當你將一門經驗技藝體制化時，你就拔掉了它的牙齒。大學體系教導學生如何用可接受的現代學術語言談論中醫，但它系統性地過濾掉了古人所理解的、粗糙而真實的物理真相：生命即是火，寒冷即是死亡，而為了追逐企業便利而無視基本的熱力學定律，絕對是一條通往毀滅的直線。
