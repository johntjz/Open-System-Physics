### 1. Pathomechanism of True Cold Below and False Heat Above

In Classical Chinese Medicine (*Shanghan Lun* and *Jin Gui Yao Lue*), clinical presentations marked by upper-body inflammation, bleeding mucosal ulcerations, or unanchored heat are frequently misdiagnosed as true excess heat or Yin deficiency. 
- **The Core Dynamic (*Shang Re Xia Han* 上熱下寒):** When the Middle Jiao (Spleen and Stomach) is severely deficient and cold, it loses its ability to anchor and contain the body's foundational Yang. 
- **Floating Ministerial Fire (*Xiang Huo Shang Fu* 虛火上浮):** Unanchored Ministerial Fire is squeezed out of the lower burner by interior cold, floating upward along meridian pathways to erupt in the oral cavity as chronic, bleeding mouth ulcers.

### 2. The Failure of Bitter-Cold Purging vs. The Success of Root-Warming

Attempting to extinguish false upper heat with heavy bitter-cold agents (*Huang Qin*, *Huang Lian*, or cooling Yin tonics) creates a severe iatrogenic trap:
- **Choking the Middle Pivot:** Bitter-cold herbs further injure deficient Spleen Yang, trapping damp-heat in the upper burner like a pressure cooker and causing rapid relapse the moment medication ceases.
- **Root-Warming and Fire-Anchoring (*Yin Huo Gui Yuan* 引火歸元):** Restoring the system requires rebuilding Earth via Middle-Jiao warming agents (*Gan Jiang*, *Bai Zhu*) and utilizing warm, acrid channel-unblockers (*Gui Zhi*, *Gui Pi*) as a magnet to gently guide floating fire back down to its root in the lower burner.

### 1. 上熱下寒與虛火上浮之病機

在傳統中醫學（《傷寒論》與《金匱要略》）中，表現為上焦發炎、黏膜潰瘍出血或熱象不定的臨床表現，經常被誤診為真實實熱或陰虛火旺。
- **核心動態（上熱下寒）：** 當中焦（脾胃）極度虛寒時，便失去錨定與收納基礎陽氣之能。
- **虛火上浮（相火上浮）：** 無根之相火被內寒自下焦擠壓而出，沿著經脈上浮，在口腔中爆發為慢性且反覆出血的口瘡。

### 2. 苦寒清熱之弊與溫中引火之功

企圖以重劑苦寒藥（黃芩、黃連或寒涼滋陰藥）撲滅假上熱，會造成嚴重的醫源性陷阱：
- **窒息中焦樞紐：** 苦寒藥進一步損傷本已虛弱的脾陽，將濕熱困在如同壓力鍋般的中上焦，導致停藥即復發。
- **溫中引火歸元：** 恢復系統平衡需要透過中焦溫陽藥（乾薑、白朮）重建脾土，並運用溫辛通絡之品（桂枝、桂皮）如磁石般將浮越之火溫和引導回下焦之根本。
