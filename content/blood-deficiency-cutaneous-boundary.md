### 1. Pathomechanism of the "Dirty Skin" Sensation

In Classical Chinese Medicine (CCM), a sensation that the skin is perpetually contaminated—compelling the individual to wear gloves as a physical barrier—reflects a profound disruption along the *Taiyin–Yangming* axis. 
- **The Prosthetic Boundary:** Metal governs the skin and body hair (*Pi Mao* 皮毛), serving as the physical and sensory boundary between self and external environment. However, Metal cannot moisten or seal itself without foundational support: Ying-Blood and Nutritive Qi percolate through subcutaneous interstices (*Cou Li* 腠理) to warm the dermis, while Large Intestine fluids and lipids (*Jin-Gao* 津膏) rise from the digestive tract to form an intact hydrolipidic film.
- **Micro-Collateral Emptiness and Unanchored Po:** When Blood and Yin fluids are deficient in a slender constitution, minute cutaneous collaterals (*Sun Luo*) run dry, generating micro-wind paresthesia (*Xue Xu Feng Zao* 血虛風燥). Simultaneously, the Corporeal Soul (*Po* 魄), housed within the Metal element, loses its somatic anchor. This tactile hyper-vigilance translates somatic dryness into an existential sensation of uncleanliness.

### 2. Classical Herbal Architecture (Jing Fang Strategy)

Treating emaciated constitutions prone to Spleen deficiency requires avoiding heavy, cloying Yin tonics (such as high-dose *Shu Di Huang*), which stagnate the Middle Jiao in humid environments. Instead, the classical strategy combines Middle-Jiao Blood generation with Ying-Wei propulsion and seed-lipid lubrication:
- **Ying-Wei Cutaneous Engine:** Combines *Dang Gui Bu Xue Tang* with *Huang Qi Gui Zhi Wu Wu Tang* (*Huang Qi*, *Dang Gui*, *Gui Zhi*) to rebuild defensive boundaries and propel nutritive blood to the extremities.
- **The Jian Zhong Blood Generator:** Utilizes high-dose *Bai Shao* paired with *Zhi Gan Cao* and *Sheng Jiang* (*Shao Yao Gan Cao Tang* chassis) to generate Yin-Blood (*Suan Gan Hua Yin* 酸甘化陰) and soften Liver Wood.
- **Large Intestine Seed Triad:** Incorporates botanical lipids (*Huo Ma Ren*, *Xing Ren*, *Bai Zi Ren*) to nourish parched Yangming Large Intestine dryness without inducing dampness.

### 3. Precision Formula Adjustments

When executing classical chassis adjustments (such as *Huang Qi Jian Zhong Tang* combined with *Li Zhong Tang* elements), precise herbal ratios prevent iatrogenic drying:
- **The Almond Distinction:** Culinary nuts (*Bian Tao Ren* / California sweet almonds) lack the pore-opening (*Cou Li*) and descending properties of classical apricot kernels. Crushed *Bei Xing* (bitter apricot kernel) and *Nan Xing* (sweet apricot kernel) must be utilized to vent Lung Qi and moisten Large Intestine dryness.
- **The Ginger Pivot:** While interior-holding *Gan Jiang* warms Middle Jiao cold, prolonged use in thin, blood-deficient constitutions can evaporate thin fluids (*Jin*). Transitioning to outward-moving *Sheng Jiang* (fresh ginger) alongside *Gui Zhi* and *Da Zao* ensures proper delivery of Yin-Blood to the dermal boundary.
- **The Necessity of *Dang Gui*:** High-dose *Bai Shao* astringes Yin inward; pairing it with *Dang Gui* actively generates new liquid Blood, lubricates the bowels, and drives nutritive humors outward to the skin.

### 1. 「皮膚骯髒感」之病機

在傳統中醫學中，皮膚持續感到受污染——迫使患者佩戴手套作為實體屏障——反映了太陰—陽明軸心的嚴重失調。
- **代償性防禦邊界：** 金行主皮毛，作為自我與外界的物理及感官邊界。然而，金無自身之水，全賴根本支援：營血與營氣滲透皮下腠理以溫養真皮，而大腸津液與脂質（津膏）則自消化道上行，形成完整的皮脂水膜。
- **孫絡空虛與魄神無依：** 當消瘦體質之血與陰液虧損時，細微皮膚孫絡（孫絡空虛）乾涸，産生血虛生風的微風感覺異常。同時，藏於金行之中的魄神失去軀體錨定。這種觸覺過敏將體表乾燥轉化為對不潔感的 existential 體驗。

### 2. 經方草藥配伍架構

治療消瘦且易脾虛之體質，需避免滋膩陰藥（如大劑量熟地黃），以免在潮濕環境中滯礙中焦。反之，經典策略結合中焦生血、營衛推動與種子脂質潤燥：
- **營衛肌表引擎：** 結合當歸補血湯與黃芪桂枝五物湯（黃芪、當歸、桂枝），重建衛氣邊界並將營血推進至四肢末梢。
- **建中生血器：** 運用大劑量白芍配伍炙甘草與生薑（芍藥甘草湯架構），酸甘化陰以生血，並柔肝木以防壓抑之木再勒脾土。
- **大腸潤燥種子組：** 納入植物脂質（火麻仁、杏仁、柏子仁），滋養消瘦患者乾澀的手陽明大腸而不生濕滯。

### 3. 精確方劑調整

在執行經方架構調整（如黃芪建中湯合理中湯）時，精確的藥味比例可防止醫源性乾燥：
- **杏仁之辨：** 烹飪堅果（扁桃仁）缺乏經方杏仁的開腠理與降氣功能。必須使用打碎之北杏與南杏以宣肺達皮毛並潤大腸。
- **生薑與乾薑之樞紐：** 守中之乾薑雖能溫中焦寒，但在消瘦血虛體質中長期使用會蒸發津液。轉換為走而不守之生薑，配合桂枝與大棗，方能確保營血順利送達真皮層。
- **當歸之必要：** 大劑量白芍收斂陰液；配伍當歸方能主動生血、潤腸並使營血外走。
