### 1. The Linguistic Collision in Clinical Diagnosis

When a patient reports "我得脾气不好" (I have a bad disposition/temper) to a clinician, a profound semantic collision occurs between modern Mandarin vernacular and classical Traditional Chinese Medicine (CCM) Zang-fu theory. This phrase operates simultaneously on two distinct semantic levels:
- **Colloquial Vernacular (*Píqì*):** In everyday modern Mandarin, the term refers strictly to mood, temperament, or emotional volatility. Within classical emotional mapping, irritability and anger fall under the jurisdiction of the Liver (*Gan* 肝), typically corresponding to Liver Qi Stagnation or Liver Fire.
- **Classical Technical Term (*Pí Qì*):** In classical Zang-fu mechanics, Spleen Qi represents the functional vital energy responsible for transformation, transportation (*Yun Hua* 運化), and fluid balance. A literal interpretation indicates Spleen Qi deficiency or functional disharmony, requiring warming or damp-draining agents rather than Liver-soothing sedatives.

### 2. Clinical Miscommunication and Diagnostic Traps

This linguistic ambiguity creates a mutual risk of misinterpretation in modern clinical settings:
- **Misinterpreting Literal as Slang:** If a practitioner assumes the statement is colloquial complaining about mood, they may bypass digestive diagnostics and prescribe Liver-clearing formulas, failing to address underlying Spleen-Earth weakness.
- **Misinterpreting Slang as Literal:** Conversely, anchoring strictly on the literal acoustic sound without evaluating systemic organ dynamics can lead to misplaced tonification that aggravates underlying emotional or neurological pressure points.

### 1. 臨床診斷中的語意碰撞

當患者向臨床醫師表達「我得脾氣不好」時，現代華語白話文與傳統中醫臟腑理論之間便産生了深刻的語意碰撞。此詞同時在兩個截然不同的語意層面上運作：
- **白話俗語（脾氣）：** 在日常白話中，該詞專指情緒、脾氣或性格起伏。在古典情感對應中，易怒與憤怒歸屬於肝，通常對應肝氣鬱結或肝火旺。
- **古典術語（脾氣）：** 在古典臟腑機理中，脾氣代表負責運化與水液平衡的功能性元氣。字面上的解讀指向脾氣虛弱或功能失調，需要溫補或利濕之品，而非疏肝鎮靜劑。

### 2. 臨床誤溝通與診斷陷阱

這種語言上的曖昧性在現代臨床環境中製造了雙向的誤解風險：
- **將字面誤認為俚語：** 若醫師將此言論視為對情緒的白話抱怨，便可能略過消化系統診斷而開立清肝方劑，無法處理底層的脾土虛弱。
- **將俚語誤認為字面：** 相反地，若在未評估系統性臟腑動態的情況下死守字面發音，則可能導致錯置的補益，進而加劇底層的情緒或神經壓迫。
