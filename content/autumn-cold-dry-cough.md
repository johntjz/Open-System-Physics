### 1. The Macro-Vector: Exterior Wind-Cold Combined with Autumn Cool-Dryness

Entering autumn while indoor spaces blast aggressive air-conditioning creates a textbook trap for what classical Chinese medicine terms **Exterior Wind-Cold (風寒)** combined with **Autumn Cool-Dryness (涼燥)**. Stepping from a crisp breeze straight into 18°C air-conditioning drives cold straight through open pores (*Cou Li* 腠理) into the *Taiyang* layer and the Lung meridian. 

The resulting dry, itchy cough is deceptive. In *Shanghan Lun* mechanics, that dryness is not true Heat or Yin deficiency; it is **Cold locking up the Lung's diffusing action (肺氣不宣)** and freezing fluid distribution. Because Yang Qi is constricted, body fluids cannot steam upward. Commercial suppressants and cold syrups act like pouring ice water on a contracted lung—"closing the door on the robber" (*Bi Men Liu Kou* 閉門留寇) and turning a brief chill into a chronic cough.

### 2. Immediate Kitchen Formulas (Zero-Trip Pantry Interventions)

When pantry staples are your only immediate tool, the goal is acrid-warm dispersing (*Xin Wen Jie Biao* 辛溫解表) paired with gentle moistening:

* **Thick-Sliced Fresh Ginger + Aged Tangerine Peel + Brown Sugar (生姜陳皮紅糖飲):** Simmer 5–6 thick coins of smashed fresh ginger (*Sheng Jiang*), 1 piece of aged tangerine peel (*Chen Pi*), and brown sugar in 400ml water for 10–12 minutes. *Sheng Jiang* opens pores, *Chen Pi* descends rebellious Lung Qi, and cane sugar moistens the pharynx without creating cold phlegm.
* **Scallion White + Ginger Decoction (葱姜飲):** Boil the white roots/stalks of 3 scallions (*Cong Bai*) + 4 slices of fresh ginger for 5–8 minutes to free trapped Yang Qi in the upper chest.
* **Steamed Pear with Ginger & Peppercorns (椒姜炖梨):** For parched throats, core a pear, stuff with 3 slices of fresh ginger and 5–7 peppercorns, and steam for 30 minutes. The spice flips the pear's thermal nature to warm, moisturizing the throat while scattering surface cold.

### 3. Classical Herbal Cabinet Formulas (*Shanghan* / *Jingui* Logic)

* **Gan Jiang + Zhi Gan Cao (甘草干姜湯):** Warms Spleen and Lung Yang to steam fluids upward while heavy *Zhi Gan Cao* coats the throat.
* **Gui Zhi + Xing Ren + Zhi Gan Cao (桂枝加厚朴杏子湯 logic):** Opens the surface, transforms fluids, and uses bitter-warm, oil-rich *Xing Ren* to descend rebellious cough mechanics.
* **Gan Jiang + Wu Wei Zi:** The classic open-and-close cough switch—scattering cold while astringing the rebellious cough reflex and generating fluids (*Sheng Jin*).

### 4. The 5-Minute Physical Cut-Off

To instantly halt an AC-triggered throat tickle (*Tiantu* CV-22), use a hairdryer or hot water bottle on the upper back—specifically targeting **Dazhui (GV-14 大椎)**, **Fengmen (BL-12 風門)**, and **Feishu (BL-13 肺俞)**—for 5 to 8 minutes until the upper back feels thoroughly warm and dewy, dissolving the trapped wind-cold.

### 1. 巨觀向量：外感風寒與秋季涼燥

秋季室外氣溫下降而室內猛烈轟鳴空調，正是中醫所謂**外感風寒**合併**秋季涼燥**的典型陷阱。從微涼秋風直接踏入 18°C 冷氣房，寒邪會透過張開的**腠理**直中**太陽**表層與肺經。

這類咳嗽極具欺騙性。在《傷寒論》機理中，該種乾燥並非真正的熱證或陰虛；而是**寒邪閉塞肺氣不宣**並凍結正常津液分佈。因陽氣為寒所束，體液無法蒸騰上潤咽喉。市售鎮咳劑與寒涼糖漿無異於向收縮的肺部澆冰水，即古人所謂「閉門留寇」，將短短兩天的表寒拖成數週慢性咳嗽。

### 2. 隨手可得的廚房方劑（無需特地採買）

若僅有常備廚房食材，核心目標為**辛溫解表**伴隨溫潤：

* **生薑陳皮紅糖飲：** 拍碎生薑 5–6 片、陳皮 1 片、紅糖適量，加水 400ml 煮 10–12 分鐘趁熱飲用。生薑開表散寒，陳皮降肺氣，紅糖潤咽而不生濕痰。
* **葱姜飲：** 蔥白 3 段（帶鬚）、生薑 4 片煮 5–8 分鐘，藉蔥白辛溫通陽之力解除胸悶與咽喉緊縮。
* **椒姜炖梨：** 若咽乾如砂紙，將梨挖心塞入生薑 3 片與花椒/黑胡椒 5–7 粒蒸 30 分鐘，以辛溫轉化梨之涼性，潤燥散寒兼備。

### 3. 經典中藥房方劑（傷寒金匱邏輯）

* **甘草干姜湯：** 溫中補陽以蒸騰津液上潤咽喉。
* **桂枝加厚朴杏子湯邏輯：** 桂枝解肌發汗、化氣解表，配合富含油脂的苦杏仁降氣潤肺。
* **乾薑五味子對：** 乾薑溫散肺寒，五味子酸溫收斂並生津，為經典止咳開合對藥。

### 4. 咽喉發癢的五分鐘物理切斷法

欲瞬間中止冷氣引起的咽喉奇癢（**天突**穴），可以吹風機溫風或熱水袋直接溫敷上背部——精準涵蓋**大椎**（GV-14）、**風門**（BL-12）與**肺俞**（BL-13）5至8分鐘，直至上背溫熱微汗，外寒一解，支氣管痙攣即會戲劇性消解。
