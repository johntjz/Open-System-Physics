### 1. Classical Formula Composition (1-Day Dose)

To resolve an autumn Wind-Cold + AC-induced dry cough at the root—releasing the exterior chill, warming the Lung to steam fluids back up to the dry throat, and relaxing bronchial spasm without over-drying the body—the prescription combines **Gui Zhi Jia Xing Zi Tang (桂枝加杏子湯)** with the **Gan Jiang + Wu Wei Zi + Fu Ling** triad.

| Herb (Pin Yin / Chinese) | Weight (g) | Ratio | Classical Role & Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gui Zhi** (桂枝) | **9 g** | 3 | **Releases Exterior & Warms Yang:** Opens *Taiyang* pores locked by AC chill and drives *Qi* transformation (*Hua Qi*) so warm fluids rise to the throat. |
| **Ku Xing Ren** (苦杏仁, crushed) | **9 g** | 3 | **Descends Lung Qi & Moistens:** Bitter-warm and oil-rich; stops the spasmodic cough reflex and moistens autumn Lung dryness without cooling the stomach. |
| **Gan Jiang** (干姜) | **6 g** | 2 | **Warms the Lung & Middle Burner:** Pairs with *Zhi Gan Cao* to warm cold-contracted lungs and vaporize fluids upward. |
| **Wu Wei Zi** (五味子, crushed) | **6 g** | 2 | **Astringes Cough & Generates Fluids:** Pairs 1:1 with *Gan Jiang*; locks down rebellious coughing while generating warm fluids (*Sheng Jin*). |
| **Bai Shao** (白芍) | **9 g** | 3 | **Relaxes Bronchial Spasm:** Pairs with *Gui Zhi* to harmonize the exterior, and with *Zhi Gan Cao* to stop smooth-muscle airway spasms. |
| **Fu Ling** (茯苓) | **12 g** | 4 | **Supports Spleen & Moves Water:** Prevents trapped fluids in the Lung and Stomach from congealing into lingering phlegm. |
| **Zhi Gan Cao** (炙甘草) | **6 g** | 2 | **Harmonizes & Soothes the Throat:** Buffers acrid warmth and calms local throat irritation. |
| **Sheng Jiang** (生姜, fresh) | **9 g** *(3–4 thick coins)* | 3 | **Assists Exterior Release:** Works with *Gui Zhi* to push wind-cold out of superficial channels. |
| **Da Zao** (大枣, torn open) | **4 pcs** *(~12 g)* | 4 | **Replenishes *Ying* Fluids:** Ensures warming and releasing the exterior never depletes body fluids in dry autumn weather. |

### 2. The Four *Shanghan Lun* Layers

1. **Gui Zhi Tang Chassis:** Unlocks cold trapped in the skin and upper back from walking between warm streets and AC.
2. **Gan Jiang + Wu Wei Zi:** The "open-and-close" cough switch—scattering cold freezing fluid distribution while stopping airway tickles.
3. **Shao Yao Gan Cao Tang Pair:** Directly relaxes the cramping, non-stop cough reflex in the trachea.
4. **Xing Ren:** Lubricates the dry respiratory tract with natural seed oils while directing rebellious Lung Qi downward.

### 3. Decoction & Administration Protocol

1. **Crush Seeds:** Lightly smash *Xing Ren* and *Wu Wei Zi* in a mortar before soaking.
2. **Soak:** Soak all herbs in 800 ml of cold water for 30 minutes.
3. **Boil:** Bring to a rolling boil, then simmer gently for 25–30 minutes down to 350–400 ml.
4. **Dose:** Divide into 2 warm doses. Stay out of direct AC drafts until a light, warm micro-sweat appears on the skin, signaling the trapped cold has exited the *Taiyang* layer.

### 1. 經典方劑組成（一日劑量）

為從根本解決秋季風寒合併空調引起的乾咳——解表散寒、溫肺蒸騰津液上潤咽喉、舒緩支氣管痙攣而不傷燥——此方結合了**桂枝加杏子湯**與**乾薑＋五味子＋茯苓**對藥。

### 2. 四層《傷寒論》機理

1. **桂枝湯主體：** 解開往返溫熱街道與冰冷空調所鎖在皮膚及上背部的寒邪。
2. **乾薑＋五味子：** 經典的「開闔」止咳開關——乾薑散開凍結津液分佈之寒，五味子止住咽喉狂暴癢感。
3. **芍藥甘草湯對藥：** 直接放鬆氣管中痙攣不休的咳嗽反射。
4. **苦杏仁：** 提供天然種子油脂潤澤乾燥呼吸道，並引導上逆肺氣下行。

### 3. 煎煮與服藥規範

1. **打碎種子：** 浸泡前務必先將苦杏仁與五味子在缽中輕輕搗碎。
2. **浸泡：** 將所有藥材浸泡於 800 毫升冷水中 30 分鐘。
3. **煎煮：** 大火煮沸後，轉小火慢煎 25 至 30 分鐘，濃縮至大約 350–400 毫升藥液。
4. **服法：** 濾出並分為早晚兩次溫服。服藥後 10–15 分鐘可喝一小碗熱米粥或熱水，且避免直接吹空調，直至皮膚微見溫潤汗出，即表示表寒已透出太陽層。
