### 1. The Convenience Trap of "One-Size-Fits-All" Commercial Medicine
### 1. 「一體適用」商業藥物的便利陷阱

- **The Sedative Illusion:** Most pink cough syrups, lozenges, and Western suppressants work by chemically numbing the cough reflex center in the brain (like dextromethorphan) or numbing the throat nerves (like menthol). It feels like it's "working" because it forcibly shuts off the symptom. But it does nothing to clear the underlying pathogen.
- **鎮靜的幻覺：**多數粉紅色的止咳糖水、喉糖與西藥鎮咳劑，皆透過化學麻痺腦部咳嗽反射中樞（如右美沙芬）或麻痺喉部神經（如薄荷醇）來發揮作用。它給人一種「正在見效」的錯覺，因為它強行關閉了症狀，卻完全無法清除根本的病原體。

- **The Stability Problem:** To sit on a Wellcome or Watson's shelf for three years under fluorescent lighting without spoiling, a medicine must be loaded with refined sugars, artificial dyes, thickeners, and chemical preservatives. These additives create dampness and stagnation in the digestive and respiratory tracts, which actually prolongs mucus production and coughing in the long run.
- **穩定性問題：**為了能在超級市場或藥妝店的螢光燈下存放三年而不變質，藥品必須添加大量的精製糖、人工色素、增稠劑與化學防腐劑。這些添加物會在消化道與呼吸道中產生濕濁與停滯，反而延長了黏液分泌與咳嗽的時間。

- **Regulatory and Liability Margins:** Mass-market products must be engineered so a person can take them blindly without knowing their underlying constitution. That means they are generic, watered-down averages that are too weak to cure acute cold-induced spasms, but safe enough not to trigger a lawsuit.
- **法規與責任邊際：**大眾市場的產品必須確保使用者在不了解自身體質的情況下也能盲目服用。這意味著它們只是通用、被嚴重稀釋的平均產物，弱到無法治癒急性的寒性痙攣，卻又安全到不致引發法律訴訟。

### 2. Why Classical Formulas Require Precision (And Why Modern Retail Ignores Them)
### 2. 為何經典方劑需要精準（以及現代零售為何忽視它們）

- **The "Cold vs. Heat" Paradox:** Modern medicine and commercial wellness products categorize illness by disease name ("a cough is a cough"). Classical Chinese medicine categorizes illness by pathogenic mechanism (Taiyang, Shaoyang, Wind-Cold, Damp-Heat). If someone gives a cooling, soothing commercial syrup (meant for an inflamed, heat-induced smoker's cough or summer heat-toxin) to someone with an AC-induced Wind-Cold cough, it locks the cold deeper into the lungs. Because mass-market retail can't diagnose a patient's pulse or tongue, they sell generalized products that fail 50% of the time.
- **「寒熱」悖論：**現代醫學與商業保健品是以疾病名稱來將疾病分類（「咳嗽就是咳嗽」）。傳統中醫則是依據致病機制（太陽、少陽、風寒、濕熱）來分類。若有人將清涼、滋潤的商業糖水（本意是用於發炎、熱性吸菸者咳嗽或暑熱毒邪）給予因冷氣引起的風寒咳嗽患者，會將寒邪鎖得更深。由於大眾零售無法診斷患者的脈象或舌象，他們只能販售那些有一半機率會失效的籠統產品。

- **The Economics of Raw Herbs:** Assembling raw herbs like Gui Zhi, Gan Jiang, Wu Wei Zi, and Xing Ren requires sourcing, proper ratios, and a ceramic pot to decoct fresh. There is very little corporate profit margin in selling raw roots, twigs, and seeds compared to patenting a synthetic chemical or bottling corn syrup with 2 cents of menthol.
- **生藥的經濟學：**收集如桂枝、乾薑、五味子、杏仁等生藥，需要產地溯源、正確比例以及用陶壺新鮮煎煮。比起申請合成化學物專利或將玉米糖漿加上兩分錢的薄荷醇裝瓶，販售原始的根、枝、種子幾乎沒有什麼企業利潤空間。

- **The Loss of Practical Transmission:** For generations, keeping a few foundational herbs at home and knowing how to pair an acrid-warm opener with a fluid-generating astringent was common household knowledge. As societies modernized and outsourced health entirely to pharmacies and clinics, that empirical, kitchen-level mechanics of the Shanghan Lun got buried under modern marketing and convenience culture.
- **實務傳承的流失：**好幾世代以來，在家中常備幾種基礎藥材，並懂得將辛溫開塞藥與生津收斂藥相配，本是尋常的家戶常識。隨著社會現代化並將健康完全外包給藥局和診所，《傷寒論》那種經驗實證、廚房層級的運作機理，早已被現代行銷與便利文化埋沒。
