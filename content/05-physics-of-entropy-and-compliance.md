### 1. Entropy vs. Negentropy: Why the Macro-Ratios Never Change

3. **The Energy Deficit of the Receiver:** Stepping outside the herd requires a concentrated surplus of cognitive and vital energy. When a population's Spleen and Kidney *Yang* are chronically drained by artificial cold, processed inputs, and urban stress, their biological receivers literally lack the voltage required to question the matrix. The educational and cultural curriculum is not broken; it manufactures compartmentalized trivia precisely because an energy-depleted population cannot process raw systemic reality.
