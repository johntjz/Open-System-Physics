### 1. The October Loop in Hong Kong
### 1. 香港十月的惡性循環

- It is an utterly infuriating loop to watch play out across Hong Kong every October. The city drops into a crisp, gorgeous autumn overnight, but the commercial buildings, MTR cars, buses, and offices are locked in a rigid, automated time warp—blasting aggressive arctic air-conditioning like it is August.
- 這是在香港每年十月上演、令人極度惱火的循環。城市的夜晚轉為清爽宜人的秋涼，但商業大樓、地鐵車廂、公車與辦公室卻鎖死在僵化且自動化的時光倒流中——像八月一樣瘋狂吹送強烈的極地冷氣。

- People step out of 19°C offices wearing summer T-shirts straight into a cooling wind, get hit in the neck and upper back with a silent blast of Feng Han, and then wonder why their throat feels like sandpaper by evening.
- 人們穿著夏季 T 恤從 19°C 的辦公室直接走進涼風中，頸部與上背部遭到無聲的風寒（Feng Han）襲擊，然後到了傍晚卻納悶為什麼喉嚨乾得像砂紙一樣。

### 2. The Collective Response: Peak Modern Superficiality
### 2. 集體反應：現代膚淺的巔峰

- **The "Take Vitamin C" Crowd:** Treating a mechanical, thermal contraction of the Lung meridian like it’s a nutritional deficiency. Popping massive doses of ascorbic acid does nothing to open locked superficial pores (cou li) or descend rebellious Lung Qi.
- **「服用維生素C」群體：**將肺經機械性、熱力學的收縮視為營養不良。吞服大量抗壞血酸對於打開被鎖住的表層腠理（cou li）或降逆肺氣毫無幫助。

- **The Overpriced "Expensive Shen / Fancy Lozenge" Trap:** Dropping hundreds of dollars at a high-end Eu Yan Sang or retail counter on cooling ginseng or premium cooling extracts. Because the marketing frames every dry throat as "Yin deficiency" or "internal heat" (Shang Huo), they throw expensive, cooling tonics at a cold-locked airway. That is the exact equivalent of pouring cold water on a grease fire—it seals the pathogen tighter into the bronchi and turns a two-day surface chill into a three-week chronic hacking cough.
- **昂貴的「名貴參/高級喉糖」陷阱：**在高檔零售櫃檯花費數百元購買寒涼參類或高級清涼萃取物。由於行銷將每一個乾喉嚨都框架為「陰虛」或「上火」，他們對著被寒氣鎖住的氣道猛灌昂貴的寒涼補品。這完全等於在油火上澆冷水——它會將病原體更緊密地封在支氣管內，將短短兩天的表寒轉化為長達三週的慢性猛烈咳嗽。

- **The Institutionalized Ignorance:** It is entirely reactive, institutionalized ignorance. Because modern wellness has been commercialized into buying branded boxes rather than understanding basic thermodynamics and classical pathology, the whole population just passes the same trapped wind-cold back and forth across the MTR network all autumn long—coughing non-stop while completely ignoring the thermostat right above their heads.
- **體制化的無知：**這完全是反應式、體制化的無知。由於現代養生已被商業化為購買品牌禮盒，而非理解基本的熱力學與經典病理學，全體人口整個秋天都在地鐵網路中互相傳遞著同一個被困住的風寒——一邊不停咳嗽，一邊完全忽略頭頂上方的恆溫器。
