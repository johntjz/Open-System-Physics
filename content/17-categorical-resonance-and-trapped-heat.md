### 1. The Core Problem: Why Identical Botanical Exposure Strikes Only One Host

In conventional reductionism, acute contact dermatitis (*Lan Chuang* 爛瘡 / *Qi Chuang* 漆瘡) triggered by tropical flora (such as fresh plumeria sap *Mian Zhi* 緬梔 draped directly across the neck upon arriving in a humid, high-solar environment) is labeled a random idiosyncratic "allergic reaction." Yet when two individuals are exposed to the exact same botanical sap in the exact same environment, and one remains completely unaffected while the other erupts in violent crimson swelling and maddening pruritus (*Sao Yang* 瘙癢), the external substance alone cannot be the sole variable.

> **「正氣存內，邪不可干；邪之所湊，其氣必虛。」**
> *"When Righteous Qi (Zheng Qi) is strong within, External Evils cannot invade. Where Evil gathers, that area's Qi is guaranteed to be deficient."*

An external botanical toxin (*Cao Mu Du* 草木毒) is not an absolute poison in a vacuum; it requires **internal permission** and **energetic resonance** to cross the epidermal threshold.
