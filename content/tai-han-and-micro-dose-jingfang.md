### 1. Clinical Diagnostics: Prenatal Cold (Tai Han) and the Phlegm-Fluid Engine

In classical Chinese medicine (*Shanghan Lun* and *Jingui Yaolue*), deep constitutional layers are forged through early nutritional and thermal shocks. When deep cold-water (*Han Yin*) bypasses superficial channels and settles into the *Shaoyin* (Kidney) and *Taiyin* (Spleen) layers, the baseline water-fire axis is compromised. 

As physiological Yang declines with age, the body can no longer contain that internal reservoir of Cold-Water, causing it to overflow upward:
- **The Spleen-Stomach Cold Axis:** When the Middle Jiao is chronically cold and deficient, the body lacks internal digestive fire (*Mingmen* and Spleen Yang), triggering a compensatory craving for high-temperature, scorched, or dry foods to borrow external thermal energy.
- **The Thermal Clash:** When scorching-dry or cooling foods drop into a Stomach and Lower Jiao filled with stagnant Cold-Water, it creates a violent thermal clash. The superficial dry heat steams the deep Cold-Water upward. In classical physiology, "The Spleen is the source of phlegm, the Lung is the container, and the Lung opens into the nose." The displaced cold fluid rushes up to the nasal passages as clear liquid, drips down the throat, condenses against heat into sticky phlegm, and forces loud coughing to clear the airway.

### 2. The Flaw of Heavy Cloying Tonics and Bitter-Cold Interventions

Relying on patent pills heavily weighted with cloying ingredients (such as heavy *Shu Di Huang*) traps phlegm inside the system when stagnant Cold-Water and Spleen deficiency are present. While a small amount of warming action may provide enough energy to "reduce the drag" for physical exertion, the unaddressed Spleen-water guarantees ongoing fluid accumulation. Similarly, attempting to clear upper dry heat with bitter-cold herbs (*San Huang*) suppresses Lung and Stomach Yang further, rendering nasal fluid even colder and more stubborn.

### 1. 臨床病機：胎寒伏飲與痰飲水氣之機理

在傳統中醫學（《傷寒論》與《金匱要略》）中，深層體質往往受塑造於早期的熱能衝擊。當深層的寒水（伏飲）避開表層經絡，直接深伏於少陰（腎）與太陰（脾）層次時，人體基本的水火軸心便會失衡。

隨著生理陽氣隨年齡自然下降，身體再也無力容納那股儲存的內部寒水，導致其向上氾濫：
- **脾胃寒化軸心：** 當中焦常年虛寒，體內缺乏真正的消化之火（命門與脾陽），往往會代償性地渴望高溫爆炒或乾燥之物來借用外來熱能。
- **冰炭相激之機理：** 當滾燙乾燥的食物進入存有大量寒飲的胃與下焦時，會造成激烈的熱力衝突。表層浮熱將深層寒水蒸騰上行。在經典生理學中，「脾為生痰之源，肺為貯痰之器，肺開竅於鼻」。被驅動的寒水化為清涕上流鼻竅，順咽喉倒流，遇熱煉液成濃痰，迫使氣道發出宏大的咳嗽聲以清除阻塞。

### 2. 滋膩補藥與苦寒干預之弊

依賴含有大量滋膩成分（如重劑熟地黃）的成藥，在體記憶體在寒飲與脾虛時，會將痰濕黏滯在系統內部。雖然微弱的溫陽作用可能提供短暫的體力支援，但未解決的脾水會持續導致液體堆積。同理，若企圖以苦寒藥（如三黃）清除上焦燥熱，會進一步壓抑肺胃陽氣，使鼻涕更為寒涼頑固。
