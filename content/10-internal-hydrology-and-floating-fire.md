### 1. The Hydrological Reality of Chronic Fluid Accumulation (*Han Yin* 寒飲)

In Classical Chinese Medicine (*Shanghan Lun* and *Jin Gui Yao Lue*), systemic water-metabolism failure driven by foundational Spleen and Kidney Yang deficiency results in the pathological accumulation of un-metabolized fluid-rheum (*Han Yin* 寒飲):
* **The Sinking of Unmetabolized Fluids:** When the core thermal engine (*Mingmen* and Spleen Yang) fails to execute normal *Qi Hua* (氣化 — vapor-phase transformation), un-metabolized water naturally follows gravity, pooling in deep interstitial tissues, lower extremities, and vulnerable mucosal spaces.
* **The Exhaust-Pipe Trap vs. Engine Restoration:** Relying exclusively on localized interventions (such as mechanical cupping or superficial drainage) functions purely as an external pressure-relief valve. It acts like bailing water out of a leaking vessel without sealing the hull or restoring systemic metabolic vaporization. True classical resolution requires restoring the central warming pivot (*Gan Jiang*, *Bai Zhu*), unblocking fluid pathways (*Fu Ling*), and guiding water downward through normal physiological channels.

### 2. Pathomechanism of "True Cold Below and False Heat Above" (*Shang Re Xia Han* 上熱下寒)

Clinical presentations marked by stubborn upper-body inflammation, bleeding mouth ulcers, sore throats, or unanchored heat are frequently misdiagnosed by conventional and modern frameworks as true excess heat or Yin deficiency:
* **The Core Dynamic:** When the Middle Jiao (Spleen and Stomach) is severely deficient and cold, it loses its ability to anchor and contain foundational Yang. 
* **Floating Ministerial Fire (*Xiang Huo Shang Fu* 虛火上浮):** Unanchored Ministerial Fire is squeezed out of the lower burner by interior cold, floating upward along meridian pathways to erupt in the oral cavity as chronic, bleeding ulcers. Attempting to extinguish this false upper heat with heavy bitter-cold agents (*Huang Lian*, *Huang Qin*, or cooling Yin tonics) creates a severe iatrogenic trap: it further destroys deficient Spleen Yang, trapping damp-heat in the upper burner like a pressure cooker.

Restoring the system requires rejecting bitter-cold purges and executing exact root-warming vectors:

### 1. 慢性水飲蓄積之水液代謝現實（寒飲）

在傳統中醫學（《傷寒論》與《金匱要略》）中，由根本脾腎陽虛所驅動的系統性水液代謝失調，會導致未代謝之水濕寒飲（**寒飲**）的病理性積聚：
* **未代謝津液之下沉：** 當核心熱力引擎（**命門**與**脾陽**）無法執行正常的氣化（蒸騰氣化）時，未經代謝的水分自然順應重力，匯聚於深層間質組織、下肢與脆弱的黏膜空隙中。
* **排氣管陷阱與引擎修復之對比：** 依賴局部的外在介入（如機械性拔罐或表面引流）本質上僅充當外在的壓力釋放閥，猶如從破損船隻中舀水卻未修補船體。真正的古典解決方案要求恢復中焦溫暖樞紐（乾薑、白朮）、以精準利水藥開通水路（茯苓），並將水分導向正常生理通道向下排出。

### 2. 「上熱下寒」之病機（真寒假熱）

表現為頑固的上焦發炎、反覆口腔潰瘍出血、咽痛或熱象不定的臨床表現，經常被現代框架誤診為真實實熱或陰虛火旺：
* **核心動態：** 當中焦（脾胃）極度虛寒時，便失去錨定與收納基礎陽氣之能。
* **相火上浮：** 無根之相火被下焦的內寒自底層擠壓而出，沿著經脈上浮，在口腔與咽喉中爆發為慢性潰瘍。企圖以重劑苦寒藥（黃連、黃金或寒涼滋陰藥）撲滅此假上熱，會造成嚴重的醫源性陷阱——進一步損傷本已虛弱的脾陽，將濕熱如同壓力鍋般死鎖在上焦，導致停藥即復發。
