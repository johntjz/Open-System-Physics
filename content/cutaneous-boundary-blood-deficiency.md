### 1. Pathomechanism of the "Dirty Skin" Sensation
### 1. 「皮膚骯髒感」之病機

In classical thermodynamics, the Metal axis (Lung and Large Intestine) governs the skin and body hair (*Pi Mao*). However, Metal possesses no intrinsic fluid; it relies entirely on Ying-Blood and nutritive lipid humors (*Gao-Jin*) generated by the Middle Jiao (Spleen-Stomach axis) to irrigate the minute cutaneous collaterals (*Sun Luo*).

在古典熱力學架構中，金軸（肺與大腸）主皮毛。然而，金本無津，全賴中焦（脾胃軸）所化生之營血與膏津，以滋養微細之皮部孫絡。

When Blood and fluids are depleted, the cutaneous interstices (*Cou Li*) run dry. This under-perfusion generates empty-wind sensory static on the epidermis. Constitutional Metal dynamics, governing the somatic filter of purity versus contamination, interpret this raw sensory static as external defilement—leading to the behavioral use of physical barriers like gloves.

當血津虧虛，腠理失於濡潤而乾涸。此種灌注不足會於表皮產生虛風感官雜訊。體質之金軸動態主導著清濁之體感篩選，會將此原始感官雜訊解讀為外部污穢——進而導向使用手套等物理屏障之行為習慣。
