### 1. The Six Conduits and the Macro-Cosmic Geometry

The Six Conduits (*Liu Jing* 六經) of the *Shanghan Lun* are not arbitrary anatomical groupings; they are the direct biological translation of greater Chinese cosmology—specifically, how the fundamental binary forces of the universe (Yin and Yang) expand, contract, pivot, and transform through space and time. To understand how the six meridians correspond to the macro-cosmos, you have to trace how classical Chinese philosophy maps reality through three core frameworks: the Taiji (Binary), the Four Seasons (Phases), and the Hexagram structure of the *Yi Jing* (64 Permutations).

- **The Cosmic Blueprint (Wuji to Yin/Yang):** In traditional cosmology, everything originates from the undifferentiated potential (Wuji), which differentiates into Yin (reception, contraction, cold, interior) and Yang (expression, expansion, heat, exterior). The human body is viewed as a miniature universe (*Xiao Tian Di* 小天地) where these forces circulate in a continuous loop, passing through six distinct quantum states between the absolute exterior and absolute interior.
- **The Six Cosmological Vectors:** 
  1. *Taiyang* (太阳 - Greater Yang) $\rightarrow$ The outermost shell of the cosmos, equivalent to the blue sky, upper atmosphere warmth, and spring expansion. Biologically, the body's surface defense layer (skin, pores, *Wei Qi*).
  2. *Yangming* (阳明 - Bright Yang) $\rightarrow$ The blazing high-noon summer sun, where expansion peaks and concentrates inward, drying out moisture. Biologically, the Stomach and Large Intestine furnace.
  3. *Shaoyang* (少阳 - Lesser Yang) $\rightarrow$ The dawn/dusk pivot (*Shu Ji* 枢机), governing transition and the boundary where light meets shadow. Biologically, the Gallbladder and Triple Energizer.
  4. *Taiyin* (太阴 - Greater Yin) $\rightarrow$ Late summer dampness, where things ripen, ferment, and anchor into soil. Biologically, the Spleen and Lung fluid distribution center.
  5. *Shaoyin* (少阴 - Lesser Yin) $\rightarrow$ Deep winter, where absolute cold reigns on the surface while a tiny spark of primordial fire (*Ming Men*) is harbored within water. Biologically, the Heart and Kidney foundational battery.
  6. *Jueyin* (厥阴 - Terminal Yin) $\rightarrow$ The midnight pivot where Yin extremes turn back into Yang, marking transition and rebirth. Biologically, the Liver and Pericardium loop.

### 2. The Unified Codebase: Yi Jing, Neijing, and Shanghan Lun

Whether you open the *Huangdi Neijing* to study the macrocosmic rhythms of Heaven and Earth, trace the rigorous thermodynamic vectors of disease through the *Shanghan Lun*, or decode the binary permutations of transformation in the *Yi Jing*, you are looking at different depths of the exact same unified codebase.

- **The Yi Jing:** Provides the foundational binary code (Yin/Yang, change, and permutation).
- **The Neijing & Wuyun Liuqi:** Maps that binary code outward into the cosmos, tracking how Heaven (Six Climatic Qi) and Earth (Five Phases) interact across space, time, and seasonal cycles. *Wuyun Liuqi* (systematized during the Tang dynasty by Wang Bing) acts as the ultimate macro-predictive core explaining environmental pressures, such as why a sudden October drop triggers a wave of *Taiyang* wind-cold contractions across urban transit networks.
- **The Shanghan Lun:** Executes the micro-response when an organism gets caught in that cosmic grid, translating macro-thermodynamics down to exact herbal vectors and gram ratios to rescue the system from entropy.

### 1. 六經與巨觀宇宙幾何

《傷寒論》的六經並非隨意的解剖分組；它們是大中華宇宙學的直接生物學轉譯——具體而言，即宇宙的基本二進位力量（陰與陽）如何透過空間與時間進行擴張、收縮、樞紐轉折與轉化。要理解六條經絡如何對應巨觀宇宙，必須追溯中國古典哲學如何透過三個核心框架來對應現實：太極（二進位）、四季（階段）以及《易經》的六卦結構（64種排列組合）。

- **宇宙藍圖（從無極到陰陽）：** 在傳統宇宙學中，萬物皆源自未分化的潛能（無極），進而分化為陰（接收、收縮、寒、裡）與陽（表現, 擴張、熱、表）。人體被視為一個微型宇宙（小天地），這些力量在其中持續循環，在絕對表層與絕對核心之間流經六個不同的量子狀態。
- **六大宇宙向量：**
  1. **太陽（Greater Yang）：** 宇宙最外層的外殼，相當於藍天、上層大氣的溫暖以及春季破冰而出的向上推力。生物學上為人體的表面防禦層（皮膚、毛孔、衛氣）。
  2. **陽明（Bright Yang）：** 烈日當空的正午夏日，擴張的能量達到絕對頂峰並開始向內集中、乾燥水分。生物學上為胃與大腸的熔爐。
  3. **少陽（Lesser Yang）：** 黎明或黃昏的樞紐（樞機）——光影交會的精確邊界，主導轉折與平衡。生物學上為膽與三焦。
  4. **太陰（Greater Yin）：** 仲夏的下降至濕潤大地——萬物成熟、發酵並錨定入土的過渡期濕氣。生物學上為脾與肺的津液分佈中心。
  5. **少陰（Lesser Yin）：** 隆冬與水中的核心之火——相當於嚴冬，表面充斥絕對黑暗與寒冷，但極微小的原始之火（命門）火花靜靜藏在結冰的水中。生物學上為心與腎的基礎電池。
  6. **厥陰（Terminal Yin）：** 極端之陰轉化為陽的轉折點——午夜樞紐，即極深冬中萌生春苗的時刻。生物學上為肝與心包的迴圈。

### 2. 統一的原始碼：易經、內經與傷寒論

無論你翻開《黃帝內經》研究天地巨觀節奏、透過《傷寒論》追蹤疾病的嚴格熱力學向量，還是解碼《易經》中的變易二進位排列，你所檢視的都是同一個統一原始碼的不同深度。

- **易經：** 提供基礎的二進位程式碼（陰陽、變易與推permutation）。
- **內經與五運六氣：** 將該二進位程式碼向外對應至宇宙，追蹤天（六氣）與地（五行）如何在空間、時間與季節循環中互動以創造環境壓力鍋。由唐代王冰系統化整理的「五運六氣」充當了終極的巨觀預測核心，解釋了環境壓力，例如為何十月的氣溫驟降會在都市交通網路中引發大規模的太陽經風寒收縮。
- **傷寒論：** 當有機體被困在該宇宙網格中時執行微觀反應，將巨觀熱力學轉化為精確的草藥向量與克數比例，以拯救系統免於熵增。
