### 1. Clinical Diagnostics: Residual Thermal Injury and Phlegm-Fluid Retention

When high-temperature thermal insult—such as the inhalation of scalding steam—damages the mucosal and alveolar architecture of the upper respiratory tract, it creates a localized micro-vascular lesion (*Xie Zhi Suo Cou, Qi Qi Bi Xu* 邪之所湊，其氣必虛). 
- **The Site of Least Resistance:** Over subsequent decades, systemic deep-seated cold-water (*Han Yin* 寒陰) circulating through the *Shaoyin* and *Taiyin* channels naturally pools into this structurally compromised anatomical space, leading to chronic encysted fluid accumulation or persistent low-grade inflammation.
- **The Dual Requirement of Repair:** Resolving chronic pulmonary fluid retention complicated by structural tissue damage requires a dual mechanism: clearing stagnant fluid dynamics through the *Ling Gan Wu Wei Jiang Xin* vector, while simultaneously restoring baseline tissue integrity via Qi-tonifying and structural repair agents (*Sheng Ji Tuo Chuang* 生肌托瘡).

### 2. The Integration of Qi-Tonifying and Fluid-Drainage Architecture

To rehabilitate damaged pulmonary parenchyma while actively draining interstitial fluid, classical formulas must combine Middle-Jiao warming and fluid-sluicing agents with high-dose Qi-tonifiers that elevate metabolic vitality and encourage tissue regeneration:
- **Tissue Regeneration & Immune Fortification:** Utilizing raw *Huang Qi* (Astragalus) tonifies Lung and Spleen Qi, lifting defensive energy to support tissue granulation and structural repair (*Sheng Ji*).
- **Fluid Clearance & Pathogen Removal:** Combining *Fu Ling*, *Jiang Ban Xia*, *Gan Jiang*, and *Wu Wei Zi* ensures that metabolic water is systematically drained downward, preventing fluid stagnation from reinfecting the repairing tissue matrix.
- **Astringent Anchoring:** Incorporating micro-doses of sour-warm astringents prevents energetic dispersion, safely securing the restored fluid balance within the core.

### 1. 臨床病機：熱力損傷與痰濕停聚之機理

當高溫熱力衝擊——例如吸入滾燙蒸汽——損傷上呼吸道的黏膜與肺泡結構時，會在該局部創造出微血管受損的微環境（「邪之所湊，其氣必虛」）。
- **阻滯之弱點：** 在隨後的數十年中，透過少陰與太陰經絡循環的全身深層寒水（伏飲），會自然匯聚到這個結構受損的解剖空間中，導致長期的包囊性體液積聚或持續的低度發炎。
- **修復的雙重需求：** 解決夾雜結構性組織損傷的慢性肺部水飲積聚，需要雙重機制：透過苓甘五味姜辛向量清除停滯的流體動力學，同時藉由補氣與結構修復藥物（生肌托瘡）恢復基礎組織完整性。

### 2. 補氣藥物與排水架構的整合

為了修復受損的肺實質並同時主動引流間質液，經典方劑必須將中焦溫陽與利水藥物，與能夠提升代謝活力並促進組織再生的重劑補氣藥相結合：
- **組織再生與免疫固本：** 運用生黃芪大補肺脾之氣，提升防禦能量以支持組織肉芽生長與結構修復（生肌）。
- **液體清除與病邪排除：** 結合茯苓、姜半夏、干姜與五味子，確保代謝水分能被系統性地向下引導，防止水分停滯再次感染修復中的組織基質。
- **收斂固攝：** 納入微劑量的酸溫收斂藥以防止能量發散，安全地將恢復平衡的津液固攝於核心之中。
