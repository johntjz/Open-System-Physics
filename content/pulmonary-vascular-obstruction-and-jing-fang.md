### 1. Critique of Modern Modifications in Pulmonary-Vascular Obstruction

In clinical presentations where cardiopulmonary vessels are compromised and oxygen exchange is heavily dependent on supplemental support, modern modifications frequently default to heavy, cloying Qi-tonics combined with bitter-cold Yin-nourishing ingredients (such as massive doses of tortoise shell, *Zhi Mu*, and *Di Gu Pi*). 

From the perspective of grandmaster-level Classical Chinese Medicine (*Shanghan Zabing Lun* and *Huangdi Neijing*), this approach contains critical pathological errors:
- **Extinguishing Chest and Spleen Yang:** Introducing heavy, cold, and sticky substances into a constitution marked by congenital Earth deficiency and fluid stasis suppresses the Heart-Lung Zong Qi and freezes Spleen-Kidney Yang.
- **The Mechanism of Abdominal Distension:** Massive doses of sweet, cloying tonics combined with cold, slippery herbs paralyze Spleen transportation (*Pi Shi Jian Yun*), trapping fluid and Qi in the middle burner. This produces pathological abdominal distension that mechanically compresses the diaphragm from below, worsening hypoxemia.

### 2. The Resolution of Classical Taboos: Processed Aconite and Pinellia

In standard pharmaceutical protocols, the combination of *Fu Zi* (Aconite) and *Ban Xia* (Pinellia) is often avoided due to traditional incompatibilities (*Shi Ba Fan* 十八反). However, grandmaster clinical application distinguishes strictly between raw, toxic components and thoroughly processed equivalents (*Pao Fu Zi* and *Fa Ban Xia*).

When treated with rigorous processing (*Paozhi*), balanced by high-dose *Gan Jiang* and *Zhi Gan Cao*, and executed with precise vector mechanics, this pairing is essential:
- *Fu Zi* restores foundational Heart-Lung and Kidney Mingmen fire, unblocking microvascular stagnation.
- *Ban Xia* descends rebellious Stomach Qi and dissolves turbid phlegm choking the respiratory tree.

### 1. 肺絡阻塞現代化裁之批判

在心肺血管受損且氧氣交換高度依賴輔助支持的臨床表現中，現代化裁往往預設使用重濁滋膩的補氣藥，結合苦寒滋陰之品（如大量龜板、知母與地骨皮）。

從大師級傳統中醫（《傷寒雜病論》與《黃帝內經》）的角度來看，此種路徑存在關鍵病理錯誤：
- **撲滅胸陽與脾陽：** 在本屬先天土虛與水濕停滯的體質中引入重濁寒涼物質，會抑制心肺宗氣並凍結脾腎陽氣。
- **腹脹之機理：** 大劑量甘溫滋膩藥物結合寒涼滑腸之品，會癱瘓脾主運化之能，將水濕與氣機困留中焦，産生機械性頂迫橫膈的病理腹脹。

### 2. 經方禁忌之解：炮附子與半夏之配合

在標準製藥協議中，由於傳統十八反禁忌，常避免將附子與半夏併用。然而，大師級臨床應用嚴格區分生品與經過徹底炮製的熟藥（炮附子與法半夏）。

經嚴格炮製、由大劑量乾薑與甘草平衡，且透過精確向量運作時，此對藥不可或缺：
- 炮附子恢復根本之心肺與腎命門之火，打通微循環瘀滯。
- 法半夏降胃氣以消解窒塞呼吸道的濁痰。
