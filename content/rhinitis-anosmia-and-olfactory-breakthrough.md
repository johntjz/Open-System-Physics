### 1. The Pathomechanism of Anosmia and Phantom Fragrance (*You Xiang* 幽香)

In Classical Chinese Medicine, chronic rhinitis accompanied by loss of smell (*Anosmia*) and intermittent perceptions of a sweet, lingering phantom fragrance (*You Xiang* 幽香) reflects an advanced stage of *Taiyin* Cold-Dampness combined with *Yangming* sinus constraint:
- **Upper Orifice Closure:** As stated in the *Huangdi Neijing* (*Ling Shu · Mai Du*), Lung Qi communicates with the nose. When *Taiyin* damp-turbidity rises from a weak Spleen and meets external wind-cold at the upper respiratory interface, turbid mucus swells the nasal turbinates and blocks the olfactory cleft (*Bi Qiu* / *Bi Zhi*).
- **The Origin of Phantom Fragrance:** When Spleen dampness is trapped for years inside a cold, constricted shell, grain-and-water essence (*Gu Qi*) ferments slightly in the Middle and Upper Jiao. This fermented Earth-Dampness Qi (*Tu Yu Zhi Qi* 土鬱之氣) steams upward into the closed nasal cavity, registering to the isolated olfactory nerve as a sweetish, perfume-like aroma until the dampness stabilizes.

### 2. The Mechanics of Bao Jian (包煎) and Nasal Orifice Opening

Integrating upper-orifice opening agents (*Xin Yi Hua*, *Bai Zhi*, *Shi Chang Pu*, *Xi Xin*) into a warming Spleen-Lung foundation addresses both the nasal source and the lower phlegm reservoir. 
- **Mechanical Irritation Prevention:** *Xin Yi Hua* possesses microscopic, downy floral hairs that, if un-wrapped during decoction, physically lodge in the pharyngeal mucosa and provoke mechanical coughing spasms. Utilizing a cheesecloth pouch (*Bao Jian* 包煎) eliminates this artifact entirely.
- **Aromatic Vaporization:** Inhaling the warm herbal vapor prior to ingestion delivers volatile aromatic oils directly onto the nasal mucosa and olfactory cleft, accelerating sinus drainage and reversing post-nasal drip.

### 1. 嗅覺喪失與幽香幻覺之病機

在傳統中醫學中，伴隨嗅覺喪失（不聞香臭）與間歇性甜香幽香感知的慢性鼻炎，反映了太陰寒濕與陽明鼻竅約束的進階階段：
- **鼻竅閉塞：** 如《黃帝內經》（《靈樞·脈度》）所言，肺氣通於鼻。當太陰濕濁由虛弱之脾上泛，並與外感風寒交結於呼吸介面時，濁涕使鼻甲腫脹並阻塞嗅裂（鼻鼽／鼻窒）。
- **幽香之源：** 當脾濕長年深陷於寒冷緊縮的殼中，穀氣會在中上焦微微發酵。此種土鬱之氣向上蒸騰入封閉的鼻腔中，因外在嗅覺通道受阻，嗅神經僅能捕捉到自身脾肺循環上升的內在氣味，從而在濕氣消散前表現為持續數日的甜美幽香。

### 2. 「包煎」之機械意義與鼻竅開通

將芳香通竅藥（辛夷花、白芷、石菖蒲、細辛）納入溫脾肺之基礎，能同時解決鼻腔源頭與下階痰飲。
- **防止機械刺激：** 辛夷花表面具備細小絨毛，未包煎時會懸浮於湯液中並物理性卡在咽喉黏膜。使用布袋包煎可徹底消除此機械刺激。
- **芳香氣化：** 服藥前吸入溫熱藥蒸，能將揮發性芳香油直接送達嗅裂與鼻黏膜，從上方治療鼻涕倒流迴圈。
