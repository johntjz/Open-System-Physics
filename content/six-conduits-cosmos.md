### 1. Macro-Cosmos Meets Biological Phase

The Six Conduits (六經)—*Taiyang, Yangming, Shaoyang, Taiyin, Shaoyin, and Jueyin*—are not static anatomical labels. They represent the six spatial and temporal phase states through which universal Yin and Yang cycle within an open system. 

By mapping celestial climatic forces (the Six Qi: Wind, Cold, Summer-Heat, Dampness, Dryness, Fire) directly onto biological conduits, the organism mirrors the macrocosm. When environmental pressure shifts, energy traps occur at these exact geometrical boundaries.

### 1. 巨觀宇宙交會生物相變

六經（太陽、陽明、少陽、太陰、少陰、厥陰）並非靜態的解剖標籤。它們代表了宇宙陰陽在開放系統內循環時的六個空間與時間相變狀態。

透過將天之六氣（風、寒、暑、濕、燥、火）直接對應到生物經絡，有機體映照了巨觀宇宙。當環境壓力轉變時，能量阻滯精確地發生在這些幾何邊界上。
