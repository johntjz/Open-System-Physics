### 1. Clinical Diagnostics: Prenatal Cold (*Tai Han* 胎寒) and the Phlegm-Fluid Engine

In Classical Chinese Medicine (*Shanghan Lun* and *Jingui Yaolue*), a patient's deepest constitutional layer can be shaped by severe prenatal or early-life nutritional and thermal shocks. When deep cold-water (*Han Yin*) bypasses the surface channels and settles directly into the *Shaoyin* (Kidney) and *Taiyin* (Spleen) layers, the body's baseline water-fire axis becomes compromised. 

As physiological Yang declines with age (aligning with the macro-cosmological milestones of the *Suwen Chapter 1* Eight-Eights developmental cycles), the body can no longer contain that stored internal reservoir of Cold-Water. What is commonly misidentified as a chronic allergy is often the internal reservoir of Cold-Water overflowing upward:
* **The Spleen-Stomach Cold Axis:** When the Middle Jiao is chronically cold and deficient, the body lacks internal digestive fire (*Mingmen* and Spleen Yang), triggering a compensatory craving for high-temperature, scorched, or dry foods to borrow external thermal energy.
* **The Thermal Clash & Phlegm Production:** When scorching-dry or cooling foods drop into a Stomach and Lower Jiao filled with stagnant Cold-Water, it creates a violent thermal clash. The superficial dry heat steams the deep Cold-Water upward. In classical physiology, "The Spleen is the source of phlegm, the Lung is the container, and the Lung opens into the nose." The displaced cold fluid rushes up to the nasal passages as clear liquid, drips down the throat, condenses against heat into sticky phlegm, and forces coughing to clear the airway.

### 1. 臨床病機：胎寒伏飲與痰飲水氣之機理

在傳統中醫學（《傷寒論》與《金匱要略》）中，患者最深層的體質往往受塑造於早期的營養與熱能衝擊。當深層的寒水（伏飲）避開表層經絡，直接深伏於少陰（腎）與太陰（脾）層次時，人體基本的水火軸心便會失衡。

隨著生理陽氣隨年齡自然下降（對應《素問》男子八歲週期的宏大時空座標），身體再也無力容納那股儲存的內部寒水。常被誤認為長期過敏的症狀，實則是內部寒水向上氾濫的表現：
* **脾胃寒化軸心：** 當中焦常年虛寒，體內缺乏真正的消化之火（命門與脾陽），往往會代償性地渴望高溫爆炒或乾燥之物來借用外來熱能。
* **冰炭相激與生痰之理：** 當滾燙乾燥的食物進入存有大量寒飲的胃與下焦時，會造成激烈的熱力衝突。表層浮熱將深層寒水蒸騰上行。「脾為生痰之源，肺為貯痰之器，肺開竅於鼻」。被驅動的寒水化為清涕上流鼻竅，順咽喉倒流，遇熱煉液成濃痰，迫使氣道發出咳嗽聲以清除阻塞。
