### 1. Internal Frost & Spleen Paralysis

When modern diets flood the body with industrial ice, iced sodas, and cold raw elements while air conditioning chills the exterior, the middle burner (Spleen and Stomach) faces a double-sided thermal assault. Core digestive fire is extinguished, turning fluid distribution into a stagnant, damp swamp where fluids cannot rise to nourish the upper orifices.

### 2. The Long-Term Thermodynamic Debt

Repeatedly suppressing acute warnings and icing the core forces the system to store unspent cold deep into the tissue matrix. Decades of low-voltage survival pave the direct route from chronic metabolic stagnation to deep cellular collapse.
