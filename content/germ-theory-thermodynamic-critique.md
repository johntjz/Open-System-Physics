### 1. Viruses Are Omnipresent; Susceptibility is the Variable

That is the core dogma of modern germ theory drilled into everyone from childhood: "A cold is caused by a virus, not by cold weather or lack of clothes." And from a purely modern, reductionist standpoint, laboratories can isolate a rhinovirus or coronavirus swabbed from a coughing person’s nose. But modern medicine's obsession with isolating the microscopic bug has blinded it to the macroscopic physics of the host.

Respiratory viruses and bacteria are floating around in the air, on public transport handrails, and in office spaces year-round. They are baseline environmental constants. But people don't walk around coughing like clockwork in July when the air is warm and ambient temperatures match the body. The infection only takes hold when the host's defensive barrier (*Wei Qi*) collapses.

### 2. How Cold Paralysis Invites the Pathogen

When a blast of 19°C air conditioning hits the neck and upper back of someone wearing a T-shirt, blood vessels constrict sharply, and the superficial pores (*cou li*) slam shut. The micro-circulation in the mucous membranes of the upper respiratory tract instantly drops. 

Without warm blood flow, immune cells cannot patrol the tissue, and local tissue temperature drops. That localized thermal collapse creates a stagnant, oxygen-starved, cold swamp—the exact breeding ground where opportunistic pathogens can rapidly replicate because the body's local immune garrison was physically frozen out of the area.

### 3. The Symptom Is the Fire, Not the Spark

The sneezing, the shivering, the clear mucus, and the coughing spasms are not actually "the virus attacking"—they are the body's violent, desperate thermodynamic attempt to generate enough heat to burn off the invader and force the pores open to vent the chill. When modern medicine labels the virus as the sole enemy, it treats the body's defensive inflammatory response as a malfunction.

So yes, a virus is present—just like scavenger insects show up on a rotting log. But blaming the virus alone for the cold is like blaming the flies for the garbage pile. If you keep your core warm, protect your upper back from artificial drafts, and maintain strong Yang, those same ambient viruses bounce right off a robust, circulating system without ever taking root.

### 1. 病毒無處不在；易感性才是變數

這正是自幼向所有人灌輸的現代細菌學說核心教條：「感冒是由病毒引起的，而不是由寒冷天氣或少穿衣服造成的。」從純粹現代還原論的立場來看，實驗室確實可以從咳嗽者的鼻腔拭子中分離出鼻病毒或冠狀病毒。然而，現代醫學對分離微觀細菌的執迷，使其盲目於宿主的巨觀物理學。

呼吸道病毒和細菌全年都在空氣中、大眾運輸扶手以及辦公室空間中漂浮。它們是基準的環境常數。但在七月空氣溫暖、環境溫度與身體相符時，人們並不會像按時鐘一樣到處咳嗽。感染只有在宿主的防禦屏障（**衛氣**）崩潰時才會扎根。

### 2. 寒冷癱瘓如何招引病原體

當 19°C 的冷氣衝擊著穿著 T 恤之人的頸部與上背部時，血管會劇烈收縮，表層毛孔（**腠理**）緊閉。上呼吸道黏膜中的微循環瞬間下降。

沒有溫暖的血流，免疫細胞就無法巡視組織，局部組織溫度亦隨之下降。這種局部的熱力學崩潰創造了一個停滯、缺氧且寒冷的沼澤——這正是機會性病原體能夠快速複製的溫床，因為身體的局部免疫駐軍已被物理性地凍結在該區域之外。

### 3. 症狀是火，而非火花

打噴嚏、發抖、清涕與咳嗽痙攣實際上並非「病毒在攻擊」——它們是身體為了產生足夠熱量以燒掉入侵者並迫使毛孔打開以排出寒氣，所進行的猛烈、絕望的熱力學嘗試。當現代醫學將病毒標記為唯一的敵人時，它將身體的防禦性發炎反應視為一種故障。

因此，是的，病毒確實存在——就像食腐昆蟲會出現在腐木上乾枯的木頭上一樣。但把感冒單純歸咎於病毒，就像把垃圾堆歸咎於蒼蠅一樣。如果你保持核心溫暖、保護上背部免受人工風寒侵襲並維持強大的陽氣，那些相同的環境病毒就會直接從強健、循環良好的系統上彈開，而無法扎根。
