### 1. The Conflict Between Modern Academic TCM and Classical Physics

When conventional academic institutions integrate Traditional Chinese Medicine into modern medical frameworks, clinical practice frequently defaults to a rigid pattern-differentiation model that mirrors Western pharmacology: diagnosing post-surgical weakness as "Qi and Yin Deficiency" and automatically deploying heavy Qi-tonics alongside cooling, sticky Yin-nourishing agents (such as large doses of *Huang Qi*, Ginseng, Tortoise Shell, *Zhi Mu*, and *Di Gu Pi*).

From the vantage point of grandmaster-level Classical Chinese Medicine (*Shanghan Zabing Lun* and *Huangdi Neijing*), this approach creates a severe mechanical contradiction:
- **The Paradox of Heavy Tonics in Fluid Stagnation:** Introducing massive quantities of sweet, cloying tonics into a constitutional baseline marked by congenital Earth deficiency and dormant cold-water (*Han Yin* 寒飲) paralyzes Spleen transportation (*Pi Shi Jian Yun* 脾失健運). 
- **The Abdominal Distension Engine:** Rather than restoring functional vitality, these dense ingredients act like wet cement poured into a cold middle burner. They trap metabolic water and Qi, producing progressive abdominal distension (*Fu Man* 腹滿).

### 2. Diaphragmatic Compression and Alveolar Hypoxemia

In cardiopulmonary-vascular recovery, maintaining open respiratory mechanics depends entirely on the unobstructed descent of Lung Qi and the proper regulation of the middle burner:
- **Mechanical Upward Pressure:** As pathological abdominal distension worsens, the expanded abdomen exerts continuous mechanical pressure upward against the diaphragm (胃脹頂膈). 
- **Compromised Gas Exchange:** This upward compression restricts thoracic expansion, preventing alveolar micro-vessels from fully opening. Consequently, supplemental oxygen dependency persists not merely due to intrinsic pulmonary fibrosis or vascular scarring, but because structural abdominal stagnation actively blocks respiratory expansion.

### 3. The Superiority of Thermodynamic Vector Analysis

True classical Jing Fang practice relies on fluid mechanics and thermal vectors rather than generalized wellness marketing or abstract categorical matching:
- **Unblocking vs. Compounding:** When core cold-water is trapped and Qi is blocked, treatment requires opening water sluices (*Fu Ling*, *Chao Bai Zhu*), descending rebellious Qi (*Xing Ren*, *Jiang Ban Xia*, *Zhi Shi*), and warming microvascular pathways (*Pao Fu Zi*, *Gui Zhi*, *Gan Jiang*).
- **Instantaneous Somatic Resolution:** As demonstrated in acute and chronic clinical observations alike, when formula geometry matches the patient's precise thermodynamic vector, physical relief occurs rapidly and decisively—resolving spasms, eliminating fluid stasis, and restoring baseline circulation without relying on multi-week pharmaceutical waiting periods or risking metabolic collapse.

### 1. 現代學院派中醫與古典物理學之衝突

當傳統經學院校將中醫納入現代醫療框架時，臨床實踐往往預設採用一種鏡像般對應西醫藥理學的僵化辨證模型：將術後虛弱診斷為「氣陰兩虛」，並自動配置重劑補氣藥結合寒涼、黏膩的滋陰之品（如大量黃耆、人參、龜板、知母與地骨皮）。

從大師級傳統中醫（《傷寒雜病論》與《黃帝內經》）的角度來看，這種路徑構成了嚴重的機械性矛盾：
- **水濕停滯中重劑補藥之悖論：** 在本屬先天土虛與伏藏寒飲的體質中引入大量甘溫滋膩之品，會癱瘓脾主運化之能（脾失健運）。
- **腹脹引擎：** 這些厚重成分並未恢復功能性活力，反而如同將濕水泥倒入寒冷的中焦，將代謝水分與氣機困留其中，産生進行性的病理性腹脹。

### 2. 橫膈膜壓迫與肺泡低血氧症

在心肺血管康復過程中，維持暢通的呼吸力學完全取決於肺氣的向下肅降與中焦的正常調節：
- **機械性向上推壓：** 隨著病理腹脹惡化，擴張的腹部持續對橫膈膜産生向上的機械壓力（胃脹頂膈）。
- **受損的氣體交換：** 這種向上的壓迫限制了胸腔擴張，阻礙了肺泡微血管完全張開。因此，對輔助供氧的持續依賴不單純是由於內在的肺纖維化或血管瘢痕，而是因為結構性的腹部停滯主動阻礙了呼吸擴張。

### 3. 熱力學向量分析之優越性

真正的古典經方實踐依賴流體力學與熱力學向量，而非通用的健康行銷或抽象的分類對應：
- **通利與堆疊之別：** 當核心寒水受困且氣機閉塞時，治療需要開啟水閘（茯苓、焦白朮）、降逆氣（杏仁、姜半夏、枳實），並溫通微循環路徑（炮附子、桂枝、乾薑）。
- **即時體質解除：** 正如在急慢性臨床觀察中所證實的，當方劑幾何吻合患者精確的熱力學向量時，身體能迅速且果斷地獲得物理緩解——解除痙攣、消除水濕停滯，並在不依賴數週藥物等待期或冒著代謝崩潰風險的情況下恢復基礎循環。
