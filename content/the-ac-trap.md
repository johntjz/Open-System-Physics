When ambient autumn temperatures drop outside, modern commercial towers and public transit networks keep their air-conditioning locked at arctic 19°C levels. Walking from a crisp autumn breeze directly into an indoor ice-blast causes a violent, artificial contraction of the host's *Taiyang* (太陽) surface layer. 

### 2. Manufacturing Vulnerability

The resulting pathogen spread across urban networks is not an unpredictable plague; it is an engineered thermodynamic failure.
