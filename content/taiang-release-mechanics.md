### 2. The Three Mechanics of Immediate Relief

* **Releasing the *Cou Li* (腠理) Without Forcing the Head:** 
  Harsh diaphoretics or poor-quality formulas often drive heat straight upward into the temples and eyes, causing a pounding headache and restlessness. Here, the pairing of *Gui Zhi* and *Bai Shao* regulated the nutritive (*Ying*) and defensive (*Wei*) layers, while *Sheng Jiang* gently directed the expansion outward. Instead of trapping heat in the upper burner, the warmth dispersed harmoniously through the superficial channels and trunk.
* **Opening the "Closed Door" (*Bi Men Liu Kou* 閉門留寇):** 
  The cough was happening because cold air-conditioning locked the surface (*Biao*), trapping cold in the Lung and freezing normal fluid distribution. *Gan Jiang* and *Fu Ling* immediately warmed the middle and lower burners, generating a steady steam of vaporized fluids, while *Xing Ren* descended the rebellious Qi. As soon as the core warm steam reached the throat, the local muscular spasm around the trachea (*Tiantu* 天突) relaxed instantly.
* **The Clean, Even Sweat (*Wei Yang De Fu* 衛陽得複):** 
  When the defensive Yang (*Wei Qi*) is freed by the warm, acrid properties of *Gui Zhi*, *Gan Jiang*, and *Sheng Jiang*, it can finally push the trapped external cold out through the pores. That gentle, non-exhausting sweat is the physical marker that the pathogen has cleared the defensive layer rather than driving deeper inward.

That immediate relief means the trapped cold-dryness has been successfully vented, and the Lung's natural descending and moistening functions have been restored.

### 2. 即時緩解的三大機理

* **不逼頭部的腠理開發：** 
  猛烈的發汗藥或劣質方劑常將熱勢直接往上逼入太陽穴與眼眶，導致劇烈頭痛與煩躁。此處藉由**桂枝**與**白芍**的配伍調和營衛，同時**生薑**溫和引導擴散向外。熱力未被困在上焦，而是和諧地分散至淺表經絡與軀幹。
* **開啟「閉門留寇」：** 
  咳嗽發生的原因在於空調冷氣鎖死表層（**表**），將寒邪困在肺部並凍結了正常的津液分佈。**乾薑**與**茯苓**迅速溫暖中下二焦，產生穩定的蒸騰津液流，同時**苦杏仁**降下逆氣。當核心溫熱蒸汽抵達咽喉時，氣管周圍（**天突**）的局部肌肉痙攣立刻放鬆。
* **衛陽得復的乾淨微汗：** 
  當防禦性的陽氣（**衛氣**）透過桂枝、乾薑與生薑的溫辛之性解放後，便能順利將困在表外的寒邪透過毛孔推出去。這種溫和、不虛脫的微汗是病邪已清除出防禦層、而未向更深處內陷的實體標記。

這種即時緩解意味著受困的風寒秋燥已被成功透發，肺部的降氣與潤燥功能已全面恢復。
