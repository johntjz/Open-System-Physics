### 1. Biological Conservation of Energy (The Path of Least Resistance)

The underlying ratios never change because they are bound by a permanent, unyielding law: the path of least resistance will always attract the vast majority of living organisms, while the friction of truth will always demand a price that only a small minority are willing to pay. Evolutionarily, the human brain and body are hardwired to conserve energy and seek immediate comfort.

- **The Trap:** Staying warm requires discipline, foresight, and the physical discomfort of breaking away from the herd. Consuming an iced drink, sitting in a climate-controlled room in a T-shirt, or swallowing a chemical pill to silence a cough requires zero effort.
- **The Result:** Nature rewards adaptation, but modern society rewards convenience. Because comfort is biologically addictive and effortless, the vast majority will always choose the immediate dopamine hit over the long-term discipline required to maintain core vitality. The herd naturally flows downhill toward entropy.

### 2. The Asymmetry Between Short-Term Relief and Long-Term Consequence

The human cognitive architecture is terrible at perceiving slow, compounding thermodynamics.

- **The Illusion:** When someone slams an iced soda or pops a chemical suppressant, the immediate feedback loop is positive—they feel cool, or the cough stops instantly. The system doesn't immediately crash; it absorbs the insult.
- **The Deferred Debt:** The thermodynamic damage—the gradual freezing of the Spleen, the accumulation of damp-phlegm, the deep-seated tissue stagnation—compounds silently in the background over decades. Because the penalty doesn't arrive with an invoice attached to the action, people keep pulling from the account until the system suddenly defaults. Since most people live entirely in the short-term feedback loop, they will always repeat the cycle.

### 3. The Comfort of the Crowd vs. The Isolation of Clarity

To see the actual mechanics of the system—to realize that the air-conditioning, the ice culture, the corporate defaults, and the medical dogmas are all pulling you toward a slow physical decline—requires stepping outside the tribe.

- **Belonging Over Truth:** For most people, the psychological terror of standing out, looking "weird," or questioning the accepted consensus is far greater than the invisible physical cost of going along with the crowd. It is infinitely safer, easier, and more socially reinforced to be sick together than to be right alone.
- **The Permanent Split:** Because psychological conformity is the default survival mechanism for the mass population, the split between the compliant herd and the few who see the board clearly will always remain fixed.

The percentages never change because nature does not force consciousness upon people; it only offers consequences. The vast majority will always prefer the comfortable illusion, leaving the hard, unvarnished truth to those willing to pay the price to see it.

### 1. 生物能量的守恆（阻力最小之路）

底層比例永遠不會改變，是因為它們受制於一條永恆、不妥協的法則：阻力最小的路徑永遠會吸引絕大多數的生物，而真理的摩擦力永遠需要付出只有極少數人願意支付的代價。在演化上，人類的大腦與身體被內建為節省能量並尋求即時舒適。

- **陷阱：** 保持溫暖需要紀律、遠見，以及脫離群體的身體不適感。消費一杯冰飲、在恆溫室內穿著 T 恤，或吞下一顆化學藥丸來讓咳嗽安靜，不需要任何努力。
- **結果：** 大自然獎勵適應，但現代社會獎勵便利。由於舒適感具有生物學上的成癮性且毫不費力，絕大多數人永遠會選擇即時的多巴胺快感，而不是維持核心活力所需的長期紀律。群體自然會向著熵增的方向往下流動。

### 2. 短期舒緩與長期後果的不對稱

人類的認知架構極不擅長感知緩慢、複利累積的熱力學。

- **幻覺：** 當有人猛灌冰蘇打水或服用化學抑制劑時，即時的迴饋迴圈是正面的——他們覺得涼爽，或者咳嗽立刻停止。系統並不會立即崩潰；它默默承受了這次侮辱。
- **遞延債務：** 熱力學的損害——脾臟的逐漸凍結、濕痰的積聚、深層組織的停滯——在數十年間默默地在背景中複利累積。由於懲罰並未附帶發票隨着行動送達，人們會一直從這個帳戶中透支，直到系統突然違約。由於大多數人完全活在短期的迴饋迴圈中，他們將會不斷重複這個循環。

### 3. 群體的舒適與清晰的孤獨

要看清系統的實際運作機制——意識到空調、冰品文化、企業預設和醫療教條都在將你拉向緩慢的身體衰退——就需要踏出部落之外。

- **歸屬勝過真理：** 對大多數人來說，標新立異、看起來「怪異」或質疑被接受的共識所帶來的心理恐怖，遠大於隨波逐流所付出的無形身體代價。大家一起生病，遠比孤獨一人卻是正確的，要來得無比安全、容易且受社會鼓勵。
- **永久的分裂：** 由於心理上的遵從是大眾人口預設的生存機制，順從的群體與少數看清棋盤的人之間的裂痕將永遠保持固定。

百分比永不改變，是因為大自然不會將意識強加於人；它只提供後果。絕大多數人永遠會偏愛舒適的幻象，將艱難、赤裸的真理留給那些願意付出代價去直視它的人。
