### 1. The Institutional Sanitization of Classical Medicine

1. **Checklist Compartmentalization (*Bian Zheng Lun Zhi* on Paper):** University curricula transformed a living system of fluid physics and environmental vectors into rigid, decontextualized syllabi designed for multiple-choice exams (*Symptom A + Tongue B = Textbook Formula C*). 2. **Eradication of Master-Apprentice Lineages:** Historically, classical medicine survived through clinical trial by fire, where apprentices watched formulas succeed or fail in real time against harsh seasonal shifts. University systems replaced lineage transmission with career academics who publish papers on isolated molecular extracts to secure institutional grants—trading *Shanghan Lun* clinical pragmatism for academic safety.
3. **Sanitizing the Thermodynamic Truth:** An academic institution cannot easily teach that commercial air-conditioning, cold-chain beverages, and pharmaceutical symptom suppression are the primary engines of modern chronic disease without challenging corporate and institutional norms. Consequently, university education filters out raw thermodynamic reality: **life is fire, cold is stagnation, and ignoring open-system physics leads straight to systemic ruin.**
